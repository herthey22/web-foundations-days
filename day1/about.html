About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a lightweight web application designed to help you quickly capture and organize your thoughts throughout the day. It provides a simple and fast interface so you never lose track of important tasks or sudden ideas.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Type your thought or task into the input box on the Home page.
  2. Click the "Add Note" button or press Enter to save your note.
  3. Review your list of active notes anytime you visit the app.

Features

  • Fast and minimalist interface.
  • Accessible and easy navigation.
  • Keyboard shortcuts for quick actions.

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcut Action
